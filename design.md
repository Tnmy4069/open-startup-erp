# Brand Identity

CyberX represents a modern cybersecurity community focused on:

- Learning
- Research
- CTFs
- Community
- Open Collaboration

Design language should feel like:

- Terminal UI
- Hacker aesthetics
- Enterprise quality
- Minimalistic
- Technical
- Fast
- Clean

Avoid:

- Glassmorphism
- Heavy gradients
- Neon overload
- Fancy animations
- Cartoon illustrations

---
